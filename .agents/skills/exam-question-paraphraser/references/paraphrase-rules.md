# Paraphrase Rules & Guidelines

Detailed guidelines for creating question variations while preserving academic and mathematical integrity.

---

## 1. Safe Entity & Scenario Swappings

When rephrasing questions, swap narrative entities with realistic, culturally familiar alternatives in Bahasa Indonesia.

| Original Entity Category | Example Original | Safe Replacement Alternatives |
|--------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------|
| **People / Names** | Ibu, Budi, Andi | Ayah, Paman, Kakak, Rina, Doni, Pak Ahmad |
| **Fruits / Food** | apel, mangga, kue | jeruk, melon, roti, permen, pisang |
| **Vehicles / Transport** | mobil, sepeda, bus | kereta, motor, truk, kapal |
| **Places / Settings** | pasar, toko, rumah | supermarket, kantin, perpustakaan, kantor |
| **Stationery / Items** | pensil, buku, penggaris | pulpen, buku tulis, penghapus, spidol |

---

## 2. Sentence Restructuring Techniques

1. **Active $\leftrightarrow$ Passive Voice Conversion:**
   - *Original:* "Budi menembakkan peluru dengan kecepatan $50\text{ m/s}$."
   - *Paraphrased:* "Sebuah peluru ditembakkan oleh Doni dengan kecepatan $50\text{ m/s}$."

2. **Reordering Clause Sequence:**
   - *Original:* "Jika sebuah benda dilepas dari ketinggian $80\text{ m}$, hitunglah kecepatannya setelah $2\text{ detik}$!"
   - *Paraphrased:* "Hitunglah kecepatan benda setelah jatuh selama $2\text{ detik}$ jika benda tersebut dilepaskan dari ketinggian $80\text{ m}$!"

3. **Synonym Substitution (Bahasa Indonesia):**
   - "memiliki" $\rightarrow$ "mempunyai"
   - "membeli" $\rightarrow$ "membuat / memperoleh"
   - "hitunglah" $\rightarrow$ "tentukan / carilah"
   - "berapakah" $\rightarrow$ "berapa besar"

---

## 3. Child-Friendly Language Guidelines (Bahasa Ramah Anak)

All rephrased content must be accessible, engaging, and easy to read for Indonesian school children (SD/SMP/SMA):

1. **Simple Vocabulary:** Use clear, everyday words in Bahasa Indonesia. Avoid obscure administrative words or convoluted academic terms.
   - *Instead of:* "Mengakumulasikan komoditas..." $\rightarrow$ *Use:* "Mengumpulkan barang..."
   - *Instead of:* "Mengidentifikasi besaran..." $\rightarrow$ *Use:* "Mencari nilai..."
2. **Short & Clear Sentence Structure:** Keep sentence length manageable. Avoid nested clauses that confuse young readers.
3. **Relatable Contexts:** Use scenarios and objects that children encounter in daily life (e.g. toys, school supplies, snacks, games, sports).
4. **Friendly Tone:** Keep the phrasing encouraging, clear, and natural.

---

## 4. Strict Invariant Rules (What MUST NOT Change)

1. **Mathematical Equations & Numerical Constants:**
   - Keep LaTeX expressions (e.g., $x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0$, $v_0 = 40\text{ m/s}$) unchanged unless the user explicitly requests randomized numbers.

2. **Scientific Principles & Units:**
   - Do NOT swap SI units into incompatible dimensions (e.g., do NOT change meters to kilograms).
   - Do NOT modify physical constants (e.g., $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$).

3. **Answer Key Integrity:**
   - The correct answer option must remain mathematically and logically identical after paraphrasing.

4. **Markdown Option Markers:**
   - Keep normalized option markers `(a)`, `(b)`, `(c)`, `(d)`, `(e)` consistent.
